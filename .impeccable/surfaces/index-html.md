## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is his record, filled in: a Philippine-style Personal Data Sheet, the form every HR and ops person has typed a hundred times, completed with his own work. It refuses the dev-portfolio template (hero split, rounded equal cards, gradient wash).

OWN-WORLD: Pale security-green form paper, printed parts in one deep form-green ink (rules, label column, solid section bars), filled-in entries in near-black, one vermilion rubber-stamp mark for status. Archivo for entries and the name, Archivo Narrow caps for printed field labels. Square boxes, hairline and 2px rules, no radius, no shadows, no gradients. Raises: one master 12-col box grid (Swiss); fixed field set per entry (catalog); reflow by whole cells (azulejo); state as marks: ticked checkbox, stamped AVAILABLE, arrow-out on live links (cutting bench); the name comb at maximal scale (portal).

STORY: Who he is and that he is available, then proof (creatives, projects with live links), then history (experience, education, certificates); the form closes with a contact block where he signs off. Visitor emails or opens LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Section bar "Personal information" across the top of the form. Label column left, entry boxes right: FIRST NAME as a letter-per-box comb CLAUDE and SURNAME comb DAIGAN (the h1), passport-photo box with the real headshot at the right, POSITION row (title), STATUS row with the AVAILABLE stamp, STATEMENT row (description), CONTACT row holding the solid green "Email me" button and LinkedIn. On a 390px phone the email button sits above the fold.

FORM: The official boxed form (PDS), position 3 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:95abab5c13452906

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
